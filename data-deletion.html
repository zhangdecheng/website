Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: October 28, 2026

English简体中文

You can ask FLORISH CULTURE LIMITED (trading as FLOURISH CULTURE, "Flourish Culture", "we") to delete the personal data we hold about you at any time, free of charge. This page explains how.

1. How to request deletion

Use either of the following methods:

  • Email: send an email to business@flourish-culture.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  • WhatsApp: send the message "DELETE MY DATA" to the WhatsApp number you have been chatting with us on.

Please include the information we need to find your records, for example: your name, the WhatsApp phone number you used to contact us, the email address you submitted on our website, and/or your social media handle(s).

2. What we delete

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, profile name, and the messages, images and files you sent us, as stored in our customer-service system.
  • Information you submitted through forms on our website (such as name, email, company, website and social media handles).
  • Creator profile or application records we created from that information.

We may keep a minimal record that a deletion request was made and completed, and any information we are legally required to retain (for example, records relating to signed contracts, invoices or payments), for as long as the law requires. Messages already stored on WhatsApp/Meta's own systems or on your own device are governed by WhatsApp's policies; you can delete chats in WhatsApp yourself.

3. Timeline and confirmation

We will verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days of receiving it. Residual copies in our system backups are overwritten automatically within 7 days after that. We will reply by email or WhatsApp to confirm once the deletion is complete. If we need more information to verify your identity, we will contact you.

4. More information

For details on what we collect and how we use it, see our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. Questions: business@flourish-culture.com.

English简体中文

你可随时免费要求橡塔文化有限公司（FLORISH CULTURE LIMITED，以 FLOURISH CULTURE 品牌经营，"我们"）删除我们所持有的你的个人数据，方式如下。

1. 如何申请删除

可任选以下一种方式：

  • 电子邮件：发送邮件至 business@flourish-culture.com，邮件主题请写 "Data Deletion Request"。
  • WhatsApp：向与你沟通过的我方 WhatsApp 号码发送 "DELETE MY DATA"。

请附上便于我们查找你记录的信息，例如：你的姓名、联系我们时使用的 WhatsApp 号码、在网站上填写的邮箱，和/或你的社交媒体账号。

2. 我们会删除哪些数据

  • 保存在我们客服系统中的你的 WhatsApp 号码、头像昵称，以及你发送给我们的消息、图片和文件。
  • 你通过网站表单提交的信息（如姓名、邮箱、公司、网站及社交媒体账号）。
  • 我们根据上述信息建立的创作者档案或申请记录。

我们可能保留一条"已收到并完成删除申请"的最简记录，以及法律要求保留的信息（如与已签合同、发票或付款相关的记录），保留期限以法律要求为准。已存储在 WhatsApp/Meta 系统或你本人设备上的消息受 WhatsApp 政策约束，你可在 WhatsApp 中自行删除聊天记录。

3. 处理时限与确认

我们会在收到申请后 30 天内 核实并完成删除，系统备份中的残留副本会在之后 7 天内自动覆盖。删除完成后，我们会通过邮件或 WhatsApp 回复确认。如需更多信息核实你的身份，我们会与你联系。

4. 更多信息

关于我们收集哪些信息及如何使用，请参阅隐私政策和服务条款。如有疑问，请联系 business@flourish-culture.com。
