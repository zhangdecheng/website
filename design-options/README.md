## Option 2 — Global Editorial Exchange

Warm-white editorial surface with black typography, acid-lime accents, thin
rules, and a disciplined grid. It pairs an expressive display face with a
neutral sans serif and uses one large, art-directed collaboration photograph.

Implementation character:

- Most premium, refined, and strategically credible.
- Strongest expression of Hong Kong as an East–West bridge.
- Best balance between brand clients and creator culture.
- Service sections remain highly readable even with detailed copy.

## Option 3 — Signal Mosaic

Modular culture-platform direction using cobalt-violet, tangerine, sand, black,
and white as flat color fields. The hero is an asymmetrical mosaic of creator
portraits, social content, and Hong Kong textures connected by a global route
motif.

Implementation character:

- Most distinctive and youthful.
- Strongest culture and localization personality.
- Best for a visually expressive creator roster.
- Requires careful restraint in responsive layouts to avoid visual density.

## Shared Requirements

All three concepts preserve:

- “Scaling Global Impact for Visionary Brands.”
- Brand-growth pathway as the primary conversion route.
- Creator representation as the secondary route.
- Three core services.
- Hong Kong and East-to-West positioning.
- Responsive single-page architecture.
- No fabricated metrics, certifications, or unapproved client claims.
