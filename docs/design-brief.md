# FLOURISH CULTURE Website Design Brief

Status: Confirmed by user on 2026-06-18  
Source revision: Feishu document revision 207, read 2026-06-18

## Product Positioning

FLOURISH CULTURE is a Hong Kong-based global influencer marketing and
cross-border growth partner. It connects East Asian innovation and brand
ambition with localized creator networks and execution across North America,
Europe, and other global markets.

The positioning should feel broader and more strategic than a traditional MCN:
the company combines creator partnerships, paid amplification, performance
marketing, localization, and culture-first creative strategy.

## Primary Audiences

1. Global-facing Chinese and Asian brands seeking international growth.
2. Cross-border e-commerce and technology brands that need measurable
   acquisition, sales, or app-install performance.
3. Premium and emerging brands that need localized storytelling and cultural
   credibility.
4. Overseas creators seeking reputable brand partnerships, monetization, and
   long-term career growth.

## Brand Promise

Make cultural boundaries invisible, helping visionary brands not merely market
abroad but flourish in global digital culture.

## Brand Personality

- Global, ambitious, culturally fluent, and commercially sharp.
- Confident and modern without feeling like a generic SaaS company.
- Creator-native and energetic, balanced by credible strategy and measurable
  performance.
- Hong Kong should appear as an authentic East–West advantage, not as a
  decorative location label.

## Visual Direction

The exploration should combine:

- Bold editorial typography and decisive calls to action.
- Creator- and social-first imagery with genuine human energy.
- Strong contrast and generous whitespace.
- A modular service presentation that can communicate complex capabilities
  without dense card grids.
- A distinctive FLOURISH identity rather than a direct clone of benchmark
  agencies.

The supplied references establish two useful poles:

- Viral Nation: black, high-contrast, bold type, creator imagery, bright accent
  color, direct conversion language.
- Existing service reference: white editorial surface, oversized typography,
  thin structural rules, numbered capability columns, green accent.

Avoid generic corporate blue, excessive gradients, nested cards, stock-photo
consultancy aesthetics, and visual clutter.

## Website Goal

The homepage must quickly establish credibility, explain the East-to-West
advantage, show the three core services, support both brand and creator
audiences, and drive qualified contact or creator applications.

## Information Architecture

The selected concept will be implemented as one responsive scrolling page:

1. Header: FLOURISH CULTURE, Services, Our Talent, About Us, Contact Us.
2. Hero:
   - “Scaling Global Impact for Visionary Brands.”
   - Hong Kong and cross-border positioning.
   - Primary CTA: “Let's Flourish Together.”
3. Social proof:
   - Trusted brands statement.
   - Approved client/partner logo wall.
4. Who We Are:
   - From Hong Kong to the world.
   - East-to-West cross-border expertise.
5. Services:
   - Global Influencer Marketing.
   - Performance-Driven Growth & Paid Media.
   - Creative Strategy & Content Localization.
6. For Creators:
   - Brand access, monetization, and strategic growth.
   - Creator application form.
7. About:
   - Mission and Hong Kong advantage.
8. Contact:
   - Brand/creator choice.
   - Identity, company, email, budget, and goals.
9. Footer with real contact and legal/navigation links available in source
   material.

## Interaction Requirements

- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile navigation.
- Functional mobile menu and section navigation.
- Meaningful hover, focus, and lightweight reveal states.
- Client-side validation for creator and project inquiry forms.
- Since there is no submission backend, forms must open a prefilled email draft
  or clearly explain the next step; they must not claim a server submission
  succeeded.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and keyboard navigation.

## Content Rules

- English is the primary website language.
- Use the approved English copy from the requirements as the starting point,
  editing only for clarity, grammar, hierarchy, and repetition.
- Do not claim unverified performance statistics, certifications, partner
  statuses, or client relationships.
- Only use brand logos supplied or explicitly approved by the user.

## Visual Sources Inspected

- Current `flourishculturekol.com` temporary page.
- Four visual references embedded in the Feishu requirements document.
- Current Viral Nation homepage at 1440 × 1024.
- The Influencer Marketing Factory was requested as a benchmark, but its live
  site returned a Cloudflare block during automated capture. Its role in this
  brief is limited to the requirements document's description of concise,
  high-conversion forms; no inaccessible visual details are being inferred.

## Success Criteria

- The first viewport communicates category, audience, and differentiation
  without explanation from a salesperson.
- Brand and creator pathways are both visible but the brand-growth proposition
  remains primary.
- The page feels credible for global brands and culturally relevant to creators.
- Calls to action are easy to find and lead to an honest, usable contact flow.
- The final implementation passes visual comparison, responsive, interaction,
  keyboard, and overflow QA in Chrome.
