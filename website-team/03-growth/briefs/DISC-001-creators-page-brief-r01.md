## 1. Page job

Help creators who want **brand collaborations / agency-style support** understand what FLOURISH offers and **apply as a Creator** — not teach “how to become an influencer.”

Primary conversion: CTA → existing homepage contact / apply flow with **`role=creator` pre-selected** (reuse footer contact block; do not invent new fee/exclusivity terms).

---

## 2. Who this page is for (target applicants)

| Dimension | Locked |
| --- | --- |
| Platforms | TikTok, Instagram, **and** YouTube |
| Priority regions | Europe & North America; Southeast Asia; South America; plus “Other regions” |
| Follower floor | **5,000+** |
| Stage | Creators already publishing (or ready to) who want brand deals / partner support — **not** a zero-follower training school |

**Open (product to align before final apply copy):** whether 5,000 means **per platform** or **combined across platforms**. Until aligned, page may say “at least 5,000 followers” and footnote “per platform vs total — confirm with team.”

**Not for (do not optimize or promise):** pure growth tutorials, “how to go viral,” beginners with no collaboration intent.

---

## 3. Service positioning (locked)

**What we are:** brand matching + operations support + growth coaching  
**What we are not (default):** exclusive talent-management agency; zero-base influencer training school  

Use “creator partnership / creator network” language. Use `incubator` / `management` / `agency` **only** if business later approves those exact words — not in this brief.

---

## 4. Claim whitelist (value foundation)

Rebuild page body from existing **Why Creators Partner With Us** (homepage), same meaning:

1. **Direct access to global brand partnerships**  
2. **Monetization & operations support** (negotiation, compliance, payouts)  
3. **Data-backed creator growth** (performance feedback / content improvement support)  
4. **Community & supply-chain access** (community / events / factory or sourcing immersion as offered)

Rules:

- Homepage title/description and this page H1 + opening must use **consistent** wording for the above.  
- PDF performance metrics (roster size, ROI, delivery counts, etc.) **stay off** titles and meta unless separately approved with sources.  
- Conditional offers (factory tours, market-specific TAP/CAP, etc.): label **generally available vs project-by-project** only after fact table confirmation; do not expand into universal guarantees.

---

## 5. Recommended page outline

1. **H1** — Creator partnerships with FLOURISH (or close: join as a creator for brand collaborations)  
2. **Lead (2–4 sentences)** — Who it’s for (platforms + regions + 5k+); what you get (three service pillars); what to do next (apply).  
3. **What you get** — Four Why Creators points as scannable blocks.  
4. **Who should apply** — Platforms; region bands; follower floor; intent (brand deals / partnership).  
5. **How to apply** — Reuse homepage footer contact narrative: submit Creator application → team reviews by email → discuss fit. Link CTA into that flow with creator pre-select.  
6. **What this page is not** — One short line: not a free “become famous” tutorial.  
7. **CTA** — Primary: `Apply as a creator` / `Join our creator network` (Creator semantics only; **no** generic Contact Us as sole CTA).
